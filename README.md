# The Null Ledger

A public forward test of two systematic equity models that were pre-registered,
independently audited, and found to have **no real edge after trading costs**.
This site tracks them forward, on paper, to keep testing that published
conclusion — it is not a strategy, a signal service, or investment advice.

- `index.html` — front page: both models, verdicts, live cumulative records
- `how.html` — plain-language explanation of the models, the testing, and the ledger rules
- `SCSES.html` / `research_overview.html` — the S&P system hub and research deep-dive
- `paper/` — live paper-trading dashboard, account page, and the append-only ledger (CSV)
- `data.json` — the front page's data, regenerated after each monthly run

Built and maintained as an educational research project by a student. Paper
trading only; no real money is or was traded. Ledgers are append-only; locked
monthly snapshots prove picks predate returns; missed months appear as gaps.
