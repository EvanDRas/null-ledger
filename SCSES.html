How the strategy works

  • 1 · Rank. At each month-end, every S&P 500 stock is scored on 13 price/volume features (momentum, trend quality, volatility, range position) by a gradient-boosted model trained walk-forward on the prior five years, blended 50/50 with plain 6-month momentum. The universe is point-in-time — the model can only pick from stocks that were index members on the decision date, including companies that later went bust (price coverage: 89% of member-months after the two-vendor data rebuild; the residual gap is documented on the Research page).
  • 2 · Select. The top ~30 names are held — enough to diversify away single-stock luck (even the #1-ranked pick beats the index only ~51% of months; on complete data the portfolio itself beats it in just ~52%), few enough to differ meaningfully from the index.
  • 3 · Weight. Higher-scoring names get more money (score-weighted), capped at 10% per position and 30% per sector so no single name or theme can own the book.
  • 4 · Repeat. One month later, everything is re-ranked. Turnover limits and a 3-month minimum hold keep changes to ~7-8 stocks a month (~25% turnover), and every trade is charged 15 bps/side in costs. No human decisions anywhere — the same rules, every month, regardless of headlines or feelings.

    • What runs automatically

  • 1st of each month, 3:15 PM CT — full cycle: price refresh, model retrain, rebalance orders, snapshot lock, prior month scored to the ledger.
  • Weekdays, 3:20 PM CT — account page re-marked at the day's close.
  • If the PC is off at the scheduled time, the run fires at next power-on. Nothing requires manual action.

    • Manual commands

    From a Command Prompt in this folder:

  • python paper_broker.py — refresh the account with live prices
  • python buy_list.py — print the current buy list (python buy_list.py 25000 for other account sizes)
  • run_monthly.bat — force a full monthly cycle (idempotent; safe)

    • Health

    If paper\ATTENTION_NEEDED.txt exists, a scheduled step failed — read the newest error in paper\run_log.txt; the usual fix is python -m pip install -U yfinance. No file = healthy.

    Operating rules

  • No parameter changes for 12 months — losing months and multi-month losing streaks are expected behavior, not signals to intervene.
  • Judgment at 12 ledger rows; decisions at 24.
  • Snapshots and the ledger are never edited — the append-only record plus OneDrive version history is what makes it evidence.
    • Data 2003-2026; performance over 234 tracked out-of-sample months (2007-2026), point-in-time universe, net of costs: 11.0% annualized vs S&P 500 total return (SPY) 10.8% · CAPM alpha −0.4%/yr (t=−0.15) — statistically indistinguishable from the index. The +3.1%/yr alpha reported on earlier, less complete data was traced to residual survivorship bias and eliminated when delisted-security coverage reached 89%. The live paper record continues and now tests this null result. Paper trading; no real capital at risk.
