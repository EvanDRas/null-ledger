Summary

This is a monthly-rebalanced, long-only strategy: it ranks all S&P 500 constituents with a gradient-boosted model over 13 price/volume features blended with 6-month momentum, and holds the top ~30 names under conviction weighting with a 10% position cap and 30% sector cap. The emphasis of the project is research integrity: a point-in-time universe, walk-forward training, realistic costs, pre-registered hypothesis tests, and a locked, append-only live paper record. Headline finding: after full survivorship correction the strategy is statistically indistinguishable from the index. The apparent edge shrank at every data-honesty step — 25.7% → 20% → 15% → 11.0% annualized — and the final step (recovering the long-dead losers) eliminated it. That progression, measured and published, is the project's primary result.

Data integrity (the bulk of the work)

  • Point-in-time index membership -- daily S&P 500 constituent snapshots 1996-2026, so the model only ever selects from stocks that were in the index on the decision date (no lookahead into future membership).
  • Delisted-security recovery -- 240 of the 777 test-window members (31%, roughly a quarter of member-months) had no price data in the naive universe — disproportionately the losers. Rebuilt from a 994-ticker membership union: rename aliases (e.g. ANTM→ELV) plus 349 securities re-downloaded across two vendors (Yahoo, then Tiingo for the long-dead names), coverage 74%→89% of member-months. The residual 11% (92 tickers) is unobtainable in retail data and disclosed. Completing this coverage is what eliminated the apparent alpha — the dominant bias in the earlier numbers was survivorship, not costs or lookahead.
  • Corruption gates -- mechanical validation truncated ticker-reuse contamination (series continuing past corporate death) and dropped 11 series with impossible prices; every exclusion is logged.
  • Cost realism -- 15 bps/side, delisting exits charged, dividends included, execution-timing sensitivity measured at ~zero.

Validation discipline: what was tested

Nine hypotheses (the three fundamental factors count individually) each faced a pre-registered acceptance rule committed before the result was seen: two were adopted and seven rejected; a separate execution-timing robustness check (not a hypothesis) verified implementability. The discipline of rejecting most ideas is what makes the surviving numbers credible.

Live out-of-sample tracking

Since the account opened on 2026-06-30 (first tracked month: July 2026) the strategy runs unattended on a schedule: monthly retraining and rebalancing, timestamped append-only snapshots written before the scored month's returns are known (OneDrive version history provides tamper evidence), monthly context benchmarks (RSP equal-weight and SPMO momentum-factor, alongside the official SPY comparison), a simulated $100k account with whole-share orders and fees, and a monthly ledger scored at real month-end prices against SPY. Infrastructure is fully automated Python + Windows Task Scheduler with failure tripwires.

Honest limitations

  • On complete data the alpha is −0.4%/yr (t = −0.15): no demonstrable edge. The strategy currently earns index-like returns while taking 1.14× the market's risk (max drawdown −61% vs −51%). The realistic forward expectation is approximately the index, minus costs.
  • Beta 1.14: the strategy loses more than the index in crashes (2008: -37% vs -27%; max drawdown -56% vs -51%).
  • All signals are one family (momentum); tested fundamental factors did not clear the pre-registered gate in large caps.
  • 11% of historical member-months remain without price data (92 tickers unobtainable in retail sources). Sensitivity so far: completing coverage from 79% to 89% moved annualized return by −4pp — survivorship was the dominant bias, so the remaining gap is flagged as genuine residual risk to the (already null) result.
